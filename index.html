Know Before We Go

Onboard

Life on the Train

  • Day bag only, max 23″ × 8″ × 8″, small enough to fit at our feet. Checked luggage travels separately.
  • Dress is casual. Wear layers since the A/C can feel cool, plus flat shoes with good grip.
  • No Wi-Fi, and most of the route has no cell service. Download books and music ahead of time.
  • Government-issued photo ID is required to board.
  • Meals, snacks, beer, wine and cocktails are included every rail day.
  • Boarding assistance noted for Patricia (cane / walker).
Luggage & Weather

Packing

  • Up to 2 bags each, 66 lb total per person, no single bag over 50 lb.
  • Keep Canyon Spirit luggage tags on checked bags the whole time.
  • The route climbs to 9,240 ft, and early November can drop below freezing. Bring a fleece or wool sweater and a waterproof jacket.
  • Hat, sunglasses and sunscreen for the desert.
  • All four stops are on Mountain Time, one hour behind Austin.
